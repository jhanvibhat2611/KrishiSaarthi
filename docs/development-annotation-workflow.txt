First independent development batch — 20 blank information-need slots

This is a workflow and empty planning workspace, not a generated or signed dataset.
No new source is approved. Evidence IDs, questions, judgments, reviewer details and
future corpus fingerprint remain empty until actual acquisition and human review.
Use existing corpus registry, ingestion, audit, inspect/build and annotation templates.
No new acquisition service, OCR pipeline or annotation application is required.

1. Approve a bounded source list and permissible local acquisition. Record publisher,
   exact title, edition/publication date and uncertainty, URL, local original SHA256,
   source language, geographic/temporal applicability and license evidence. Publication
   date is not website crawl/upload/footer date. No open license is inferred from access.
2. Prepare a fresh versioned corpus bundle using the existing corpus CLI. Keep originals
   and canonical text immutable. Audit selected wheat pages, preserving physical page,
   printed label, page text hash, source hashes and exact Unicode [start,end) offsets.
   Render title/license and every proposed evidence page. Compare text order, glyphs,
   numbers/units and page-local visibility. Check exact and near-duplicate pages/paragraphs
   across editions/sources; suspicious repeated spreads stay held. Do not delete duplicates
   from canonical text or repair glyphs in place. Request a clean source or approve a new
   extraction version separately. Unresolved tables, private names/addresses and ambiguous
   numerical recommendations stay outside the evidence index. No blanket PDF approval.
3. Humans approve contiguous regions with recorded conditions and limitations. Use the
   current validation and approved-region build workflow, 256/48 configuration. Freeze a
   NEW corpus version; never amend Pilot Corpus v0.1. Freeze evidence-family blocks before
   question assignment, linking duplicate/related multi-span sources across documents.
4. Allocate proposed topics: varieties/sowing 3; nutrients 3; irrigation 3; diseases/pests 3;
   harvesting 2; storage 2; four explicit missing-evidence cases distributed across topics.
   These 16+4 are targets, not answerability assignments. Aim at six ALL-multi-span needs,
   three documents/regions and max eight needs per document. Missing coverage is reported.
5. Independent human authors write the 20 needs without seeing old pilot question wording,
   model rankings or generated answers. They may inspect approved evidence and describe
   genuine source-scoped needs, not merely copy sentences. Author EN/HI/natural Hinglish
   for each. Reviewers check equivalence independently; record source, crop, place, season,
   cultivation conditions, exceptions and units. Stress spelling/keyword/script variants
   are optional, separately reviewed and linked to the SAME parent group and split.
6. Second human reviewer maps minimal canonical evidence spans, IDs and page references.
   For multiple required parts use ALL, with separate rows for disconnected spans; never
   merge unrelated fragments. Evidence ID, source-region ID and passage ID are distinct.
   Re-check each language's support, not just English. Keep exact quotes local only.
7. For unanswerable proposals, inspect the ENTIRE newly frozen corpus; record the missing
   requirement and searched scope. Another document may now answer an old pilot-unanswerable
   question. Never inherit that label. Blank fields mean pending, not unanswerable. A source
   conflict may require a scoped comparison or uncertain status, not arbitrary majority choice.
8. Adjudicate disagreements with real identities/times and retained notes. Require per-variant
   acceptance, language equivalence, privacy, source-span and ALL coverage checks. Failed checks
   stay pending/revise/reject. No assistant recommendation substitutes for human sign-off.
9. Validate final hashes and exact source/page/span mappings against the reviewed corpus. Use
   the existing validated label schema and metric pathway; no claim of a general public gold-
   export CLI. The local pilot-specific export script must not be blindly reused with new IDs.
   Any needed export/report adaptation is separate, tested work after corpus/schema sign-off.
10. Only after final sign-off and explicit evaluation authorization export verified records and
    run frozen baselines. No automatic evaluation of drafts. Keep the original signed pilot
    and its old answerability scope intact. Store all new filled forms and results under data/.

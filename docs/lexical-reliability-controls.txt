Phase 5.2 — simple lexical reliability controls

Implementations
Filtered positive-score RRF reuses the existing RRFRetriever, k=60, weights [1,1],
gate_nonpositive_lexical=True and complete saved filtered-BM25/dense rankings.
The new ContentGatedRRFRetriever is a separate diagnostic. It uses the same RRF
and permits a lexical contribution only when score>0 AND the query and candidate
share at least one policy token. Original component ranks are never compacted.
No original retriever, scores, labels or result files are changed.

Content policy
Start with the existing punctuation-filtered Unicode terms. Casefold, then remove
the explicit fixed FUNCTION_WORDS and GENERIC_WORDS sets in content_gate.py from
BOTH query and candidate. The manifest records the full lists, not just a name.
Generic wheat/crop/field/farm words do not establish a distinctive content match.
Other agricultural terms, cultivar components, named-equipment words, alphanumeric
identifiers, numbers and units remain. No entity dictionary or learned classifier.
This is a lexical proxy for meaningful overlap, not a semantic support judgment.
An exact number or unit can pass the gate; it may be incidental or unrelated. All
such overlaps are exposed in per-candidate diagnostics, not certified meaningful.

Hyphenated rice-residue -> rice, residue; synthetic PBW-999 -> pbw, 999.
Decimal 12.5 -> 12, 5, as in the approved baseline tokenizer. Numeric tokens survive
but decimal/range/quantity semantics are not reconstructed. No translation,
transliteration, stemming, joining or extra embedding model. Devanagari words keep
attached marks. Crop-word exclusion is exact English vocabulary, not multilingual
synonym removal. Numerical eligibility follows the explicit Phase 5.2 requirement;
it differs from the earlier proposed alphabetic-only diagnostic.

S(q,p) = 1/(60+r_dense(p)) + g(q,p)/(60+r_filtered_BM25(p)).
Positive gate: g=1 iff score>0.
Content gate: g=1 iff score>0 and policy(query) intersects policy(candidate).
Missing/gated lexical contributions are zero. If all lexical contributions vanish,
the ordering, passage IDs, ranks and provenance exactly match dense-only retrieval,
including its existing tie order. Hybrid scores remain reciprocal-rank scores,
not raw cosine scores. This preserves ranking, not the raw dense result object.
Input rank completeness and all source provenance are validated even for candidates
that will be gated out. Invalid scores/provenance fail rather than disappearing.
The private score-mask adapter is an internal RRF input, not a standalone retriever;
it zeros rejected contributions while retaining original ranks for gating.

Inference isolation
The gate consumes only query text, indexed passage text and component outputs.
Gold labels and expected answers are never imported or read by content_gate.py.
The experiment runner uses labels only for scoring and retrospective failure reports.
Original signed queries are evaluated as development material. The 24 pending
stress variants and their approvals remain unchanged and are not evaluated.

Reproduce with authorized local assets:
  PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python scripts/run_lexical_reliability_controls.py \
    --output data/phase5.2-controls-reproduction-001
  .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q
Uses the validated Phase 5.1 saved lexical rankings and saved E5/BGE-M3 rankings.
Fresh ignored output only; no encoder, generator or new checkpoint loaded.
Configuration: configs/lexical-reliability-controls.json. All source/configuration
hashes, input hashes, dependency versions and policy lists are saved in the manifest.

Interpretation boundary
Prefer the simpler filtered positive-score gate unless independent evidence shows
benefit from content gating. Keep both dense backbones as controls; do not choose a
universal winner on this small development pilot. Score gating can remove accidental
useful lexical rank effects as well as harmful ones. Dense-only can still miss one
part of a multi-evidence need. Neither gate establishes relevance, answerability or
agricultural correctness. No novelty or significance is claimed. More complex
adaptive retrieval requires a new rationale and independent reviewed evaluation.
